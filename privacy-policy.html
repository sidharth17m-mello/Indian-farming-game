Privacy Policy — Indian Farm Game: By Roots

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Indian Farm Game: By Roots ("the App") is an educational farming game for children. This policy explains what information the App does and does not handle.

Our short answer

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us, to advertisers, or to any third party. Everything the App remembers is saved only on the device it's played on.

What the App stores, and where

The App saves a small amount of gameplay information on your own device only, using your browser's local storage:

  • Coins, level, and experience points earned in the game
  • Which in-game facts have been learned (for the Journal feature)
  • Which upgrades have been purchased
  • Difficulty setting chosen
  • An optional nickname, if entered, used only to label entries on the on-device "Top Farmers" list

None of this is sent to a server. We do not operate a server that receives this data. If the app is uninstalled, the browser data is cleared, or a different device/browser is used, this information does not carry over and cannot be recovered by us, because we never had it.

The optional nickname

The App may ask for a nickname before showing gameplay stats on a local "Top Farmers" list. This step can always be skipped. The nickname is:

  • Never required to play the game
  • Not verified or linked to any real-world identity
  • Only ever visible on the same device it was entered on
  • Not transmitted anywhere

Voice / read-aloud feature

The App includes an optional feature that reads instructions and facts aloud, and lets a player choose between English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, and Kannada. This uses your device's or browser's built-in text-to-speech engine. Depending on the device and browser, some text-to-speech engines process speech entirely on the device, while others (controlled by your device/browser settings, not by the App) may use an online service provided by the device manufacturer or browser maker to generate speech. The App itself does not send data to any such service directly, does not control this behavior, and does not receive anything back from it beyond the audio played on the device. To guarantee no data of any kind leaves the device, turn off the voice feature in the App (🔇).

What the App does NOT do

  • Does not use location, camera, microphone (other than the device's own read-aloud voice, described above), contacts, or any other device permission
  • Does not display ads
  • Does not include any analytics or tracking software development kits (SDKs)
  • Does not create user accounts or require sign-in
  • Does not share, sell, or transmit any information to third parties
  • Does not knowingly collect personal information from children, because it does not collect personal information from anyone

Children's privacy

This App is designed to be safe for children to use without adult supervision from a data-collection standpoint, because it simply does not collect personal data. There is nothing for a parent or guardian to request access to, correct, or delete on our end, since no information is ever transmitted to or stored by us.

Changes to this policy

If this App's data practices ever change, this page will be updated with a new "Last updated" date before the change takes effect.
